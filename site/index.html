How it works

Every buy on the curve, every wallet, every outcome — then the honest arithmetic.

01

Watch the whole curve

Kalita reads every launch, buy, sell and graduation on the Pons bonding curve — tens of thousands of tokens a day — and traces buys made through the router back to the wallet that really owns them.

02

Score from where you would enter

Each signal is simulated as your entry two seconds later, with the curve moved by your size. The wallet is compared with the market at the same point on the curve — a sniper is judged against snipers, a late buyer against late buyers.

03

Keep the list honest

Selection on one period, validation on days the selection never saw, a placebo list for comparison. Wallets enter after consecutive passes and leave after consecutive failures — no daily churn.

Method

Built to resist the three ways wallet lists lie.

Any list picked by past performance contains lucky wallets. Kalita's job is to keep them out — and to say plainly how much confidence each number deserves.

Small samples are shrunk, not trusted

Five hits out of five is not 100%. Every rate is pulled toward the market base and ranked by its pessimistic lower bound, so a long good record beats a short perfect one.

Position on the curve is the baseline

Early on a curve, ×2 comes from geometry, not skill. Expected hit rates are computed per share of the graduation threshold already collected — each wallet gets its own fair benchmark.

One owner, one vote

Wallets that buy the same tokens in the same blocks are clustered as one operator. Contracts and routers are flagged by on-chain code checks and removed.

Two kinds of edge, two lists

Fast: ×2 within the hour, tight exits. Pickers: tokens that graduate or reach ×5 within a day — slower, deeper, a different strategy. One wallet can be on both.

The list

A living list, not a screenshot.

    Every signal's passport: peak and time-to-peak from 1 minute to 30 days, drawdown before the first +50% and ×2, first-10-minute rug check, graduation, own exit.
  • —
    Range filters over 17 metrics; add everyone who qualifies in one click.
  • —
    Change log with the numbers behind every add and remove; quarantine for dropped wallets.
  • —
    Compare any external list — a Telegram bot's, a friend's — against the market on the same positions.
